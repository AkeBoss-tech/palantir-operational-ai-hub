# Data Gaps

Record missing datasets, unresolved provenance issues, incomplete controls, and blockers that prevent trusted analysis.

- YouTube exposed English captions for 25 of the 100 videos in this snapshot.
- Seventy-five summaries therefore rely on the channel's official descriptions and metadata,
  not full spoken-word transcripts.
- Demonstrated customer outcomes and performance figures have not been independently audited.
- A future deeper pass could transcribe audio for captionless videos, subject to compute,
  storage, and source-policy constraints.
