# Source Selection Method

Sources are ranked on five dimensions: direct relevance to operational AI, technical depth,
evidence proximity, update consistency, and complementarity with the Palantir corpus.

- Tier A: inspect continuously; likely to produce reusable technical or policy knowledge.
- Tier B: review weekly; valuable but broader, more analytical, or more model/infrastructure focused.
- Tier C: monitor for named projects and primary announcements, then seek corroboration.

Promotion into trusted topic notes requires provenance, an explicit evidence type, and a maturity
label. Vendor claims without external support remain attributed claims. Duplicate launch coverage
is clustered into one event record with multiple sources rather than stored as separate discoveries.
