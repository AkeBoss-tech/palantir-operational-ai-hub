# Evidence and Method

## Evidence labels

- **Transcript-backed:** supported by available spoken content.
- **Official metadata:** supported by the publisher's title or description.
- **Attributed outcome claim:** a result reported by a vendor or customer participant.
- **Independent corroboration:** supported by a separate benchmark, filing, paper, government record, or credible third party.
- **Inference:** an analytical conclusion derived from the evidence rather than explicitly stated.

## Workflow

The 100 videos were frozen by ID, partitioned exactly once, summarized with evidence notes, recombined, coverage-checked, and reviewed through three independent Terra passes. External ecosystem sources are categorized by provenance and bias. Volatile claims—especially regulation, product availability, leadership, pricing, and benchmarks—must be rechecked before use.
