# Palantir Video Corpus

The frozen corpus contains the 100 newest videos from Palantir's YouTube channel as of July 14, 2026.

- 100 unique video IDs with no gaps or duplicates
- 25 transcript-backed reviews
- 75 official-metadata-backed reviews
- Ten non-overlapping batch reports
- Three independent GPT-5.6 Terra second-pass reviews

The corpus is strong evidence for what Palantir and participating customers chose to present. It is not, by itself, independent proof of effectiveness or causality. Customer outcomes remain attributed unless corroborated.

The searchable catalog and detailed entries are available on the main research site.
