# Sectors and Use Cases

The video corpus and expanded research cover:

- **Healthcare:** utilization review, care operations, claims, accreditation, and patient workflows.
- **Industrial:** production planning, maintenance, quality, construction, refinery operations, and supply chains.
- **Defense and public safety:** command and control, sensing, shipbuilding, cyber defense, crisis response, and autonomy.
- **Mobility:** fleet operations, rail, airlines, racing, and advanced air mobility.
- **Energy and infrastructure:** nuclear fuel, oil and gas, telecom, satellites, and data centers.
- **Science and life sciences:** drug discovery, experimental planning, and research agents.
- **Knowledge work:** coding, data analysis, research, finance, legal operations, customer service, and internal support.

The common pattern is domain context plus bounded action—not a generic chatbot applied unchanged to every sector.
