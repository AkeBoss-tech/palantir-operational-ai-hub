# Glossary

- **Agent:** a system that pursues a goal through multiple model and tool steps.
- **Agent runtime:** infrastructure for state, tools, scheduling, recovery, permissions, and monitoring.
- **Bounded autonomy:** authority constrained by tools, policies, scope, approvals, and stop conditions.
- **Context:** information supplied or retrieved for a model or agent run.
- **Digital twin:** a digital representation used to understand, simulate, or control a physical system.
- **Evaluation:** a method for measuring system quality, capability, safety, or outcome performance.
- **Inference:** both model execution and, in research, a conclusion derived from evidence.
- **Knowledge graph:** entities and relationships represented as a graph.
- **MCP:** Model Context Protocol, a standard for connecting AI applications to tools and data.
- **Ontology:** a governed model of domain objects, relationships, rules, actions, and meaning.
- **RAG:** retrieval-augmented generation; retrieving evidence to support model responses.
- **Semantic layer:** consistent business definitions and metrics over underlying data.
- **VLA:** vision-language-action model used to connect perception and instructions to physical action.
