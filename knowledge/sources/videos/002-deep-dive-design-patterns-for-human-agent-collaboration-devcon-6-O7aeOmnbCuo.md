# Deep Dive: Design Patterns for Human-Agent Collaboration | DevCon 6

**Official video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O7aeOmnbCuo)
## Official description

Every AI product seems to look the same. Yours doesn't have to.

At DevCon 6, Palantir Product Designers Emily Su, Lide Li, and Philipp Shchekin explore design patterns that make human-agent collaboration seamless, legible, and trustworthy. They leverage the Ontology to design and encode these patterns, presenting agent reasoning without causing information overload, and building clear attribution so that users always know who made a decision.
