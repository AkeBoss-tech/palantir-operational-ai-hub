# Palantir and the NHS | UK Stories

Official video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NANvLfyDQBI

## Official description

The NHS Federated Data Platform, powered by Palantir, is driving up the number of NHS operations while bearing down on waiting lists.

Each hospital has its own version of the software of which it controls. Yet, because it is the same kind of software, they can share information with other hospitals and NHS organisations simply and securely where that can help improve care for patients.

Hear about the programme, its benefits, and the partnership with Palantir from NHS England's Ming Tang, Chief Data and Analytics Officer and interim Chief Digital and Information Officer; Ayub Bhayat, Director of Data and Analytics; Rebecca Llewellyn, Director of Data Management and Transformation; and Palantir engineers Jasmine Brown, Ethan Dodd, and Sam Rhodes.
