# Product Launch: Agent Observability & Optimization | DevCon 6

Official video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GZHSCMz6Aio

## Official description

You can't optimize what you can't see, and you can't scale what only humans can fix.

At DevCon 6, Palantir Group Lead Christopher Jeganathan and Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer Colton Rusch turn raw agent telemetry into a readable narrative with AIP Inspect and Agent Timeline. Then they launch AIP Evolve, a new product that deploys teams of agents to continuously optimize any objective across your AI systems, especially cost, quality, and speed. 

Watch Evolve cut latency by up to 90% on a manufacturing workflow, decreasing one case from 45 seconds to 8.8, by testing model swaps, prompt rewrites, and architecture changes against your own evals, automatically.
