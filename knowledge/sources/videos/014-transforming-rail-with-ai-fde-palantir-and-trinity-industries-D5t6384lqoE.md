# Transforming Rail with AI FDE | Palantir and Trinity Industries

**Official video:** [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D5t6384lqoE)
## Official description

Trinity Industries manages one of the largest railcar lease fleets in North America — 140,000 rail cars and $8 billion in assets — with thousands of repair invoices requiring audit every month. In this session, Jean Savage, CEO and President of Trinity Industries, works with Palantir Software Engineer Ankit Shankar to build an intelligent invoice auditing workflow using Palantir AI FDE. From data ingestion to ontology design to a production-ready auditing application, the entire process unfolds in minutes. Learn more about Palantir AI FDE: https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/ai-fde/overview
