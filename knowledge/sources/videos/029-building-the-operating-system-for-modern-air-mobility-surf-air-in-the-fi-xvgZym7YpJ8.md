# Building the Operating System for Modern Air Mobility | Surf Air In the Field at AIPCon 10

Official video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xvgZym7YpJ8

## Official description

At AIPCon 10, Surf Air's team walks through how they built Surf OS — a connected operating system for air mobility — on Palantir Foundry and AIP. Fuel optimization stood up in weeks. A real-time control tower now gives operations, maintenance, and flight leadership instant situational awareness. An AIP-powered pricing engine spans thousands of flights. What once took days and entire teams now takes minutes, and connected aircraft are next.
