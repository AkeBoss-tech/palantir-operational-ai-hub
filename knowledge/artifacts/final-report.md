# Palantir YouTube: What the Latest 100 Videos Are About

## Executive conclusion

The channel documents an enterprise-AI strategy built around operational context and governed
action. Palantir's core bet is that models become useful when an organization's data, rules,
decisions, and permitted actions are represented in an Ontology. AIP and its agent stack then use
that representation to plan and execute real work; Apollo and related tooling deploy and operate
the software in demanding environments.

The most important recent development is an increasingly complete production-agent platform:
Agent Engine for durable execution, Orchestrator for multi-agent work, observability and
optimization for quality/cost control, Scenarios for safe operational experimentation,
human-agent design patterns for trust, SuperRepo for pro-code development, and Apollo for
controlled delivery. In other words, the newest videos are about making agents governable,
debuggable, deployable, and useful inside real institutions.

## Evidence quality

This is a channel analysis, not independent verification of every product or outcome. All 100
entries retain their official URLs. Transcript-backed summaries are identified as such; entries
without captions explicitly state that they rely on official descriptions and metadata. Inference
is labeled rather than blended into source claims.
